# Symphorica's UGC Sources

A small collection of files for my accessories that are completely free to use, modify, and upload!

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A few things to keep in mind:
> 1. I use Blender, so everything in this collection is made to only be compatible with Blender.
> 2. Some models may be unfinished, have missing textures, or are too unoptimized to publish.
> 3. This folder contains hats already on the catalog. I won't contest reuploads, yet moderation still exists - **_so be cautious._**
>
> For questions and feedback, contact me here at my [Discord server](https://discord.com/invite/5Vq4ZYMu3g).

> [!WARNING]
> Files that end in **.lzp** and **.pdn** are textures requiring [LazPaint](https://lazpaint.github.io/) or [Paint.NET](https://getpaint.net/)

## For creators

Before you jump head-first into making UGC, it's _essential_ that you learn Blender.

There are an endless amount of tutorials that go over how to navigate the workspace, create and modify geometry, unwrap UVs, and apply textures. I can't pinpoint you to any specific one, but they should all be roughly the same. Use your best judgement.

Those tutorials will show you 99% of what you need to know. The other 1% is publishing to Roblox.

What you're about to see is assuming you have one of the accessories in this collection and wish to upload it. Again, while you're *technically* allowed to, I assume zero responsibility afterwards. If it gets taken down, it's not my problem.
> ... moderation still exists - **_so be cautious._**

### How to export, test, and publish an accessory

> [!NOTE]
> Ahead is a tutorial for absolute beginners. [Jump to here if you don't care](#other)

1. Install and launch [Blender](https://www.blender.org/download/)

2. On the splashscreen, press **"Open"**

<img width="500" height="450" alt="2" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/b1801710-06d9-4ee5-af5f-18e1f425b684" />

3. Locate your **.blend** file and open it

4. Save and export as **.obj** or **.fbx** (whichever works)

<img width="500" height="450" alt="4" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9eb59ed1-d5cf-47bf-a388-10983e2980db" />

5. Open a place in Studio and create a rig and MeshPart

<img width="500" height="500" alt="5" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/1f2ca5df-d39b-45ed-b0fc-ec71442fe9ca" />

6. Select the MeshPart and open your exported mesh using **"MeshID"** in Properties

7. Go to your Home tab, select **"Assets"** and import the **.png** texture

<img width="500" height="450" alt="7" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/16917711-2492-49db-ba11-89f90afa8a08" />

8. Go back to Properties, select **"TextureContent"** or **"TextureID"** and click the imported texture

9. Position the mesh on the rig

10. Using a plugin called [Asset Creator](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/1602528167/Asset-Creator), do as follows:

<img width="500" height="375" alt="10" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e3f1443a-176a-443d-a7e3-7dc342d22dcc" />

11. Using another plugin called [Thumb Maker](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/14034349734/), select your new **"MeshPartAccessory"** and adjust its thumbnail (press button)

<img width="500" height="400" alt="image" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/c6666b91-f437-4f4b-b050-823fd0aec4c4" />

12. Get and import [Kairoh's Accessory Testing Models](https://create.roblox.com/store/asset/79048271398967/Kairoh-Accesory-Models) from the Creator Store

13. Replace the 1.0's **"Placeholder Accesory"** with your **"MeshPartAccessory"**

<img width="500" height="370" alt="13" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/27f26f05-cd77-49d4-8083-74adacc28166" />

14. Play, test, and repeat steps 9-13 for adjustments

<img width="500" height="370" alt="14" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/600f89d9-dc1b-428f-9b6e-ce068598233f" />

15. Right-click your accessory, go to **"Export --> Save to Roblox"** and do as follows:

<img width="500" height="300" alt="15" src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/5ae900a3-9cf0-444b-94f3-030b2ea57c71" />

Congrats! You can now publish your UGC to the catalog.

### Afterword

The way I set these files up is to allow you to modify everything.

My philosophy thus far has always been to create something unique, even if it's heavily inspired by another work. All of my accessories have this in common. It's why I've never stole meshes, retextured a previous hat, or lazily copied something already on the catalog.

You can argue just by looking at these hats that I have - which is why I never once claimed these ideas for my own. It's very easy to see how much of a parody they are from Roblox's own items. I don't dispute this.

Taking an accessory and recreating it from the ground-up is very fascinating to me. Everyone has a different perspective on things, and this is my way of showing it. I loved every second of making these hats!

So, with that in mind, I challenge you to take it upon yourself and iterate on my ideas.

Or don't. It's not my call. Have fun!
